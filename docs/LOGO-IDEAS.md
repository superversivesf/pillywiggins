# Pillywiggins Logo — Brainstorm

## Core Identity

- **Name origin**: "Pillywiggins" are small fairy creatures from English folklore, living among wildflowers
- **Product**: A council of AI agents — multiple independent personalities sharing a common noticeboard, running on your own hardware
- **Tone**: Playful but not childish; magical but grounded in real technology; personal but not corporate
- **Key visual elements**: 5 agents, memory isolation, council/circle, home/local, tools/skills

---

## Concept A — The Council Ring

Five small fairy-like figures sit in a circle on a wooden tabletop. Each glows a different color — Telegram blue, Discord blurple, Slack green, Matrix amber, Email grey. In the center, a shared tablet or parchment glows warmly, covered in notes and sketches. Behind them, a window shows a night sky.

**Vibe**: Warm, human-scale, domestic magic — like a D&D party planning their next move.

**Strengths**: Instantly communicates "council." The 5 colors map to channels. Human-relatable.

**Weaknesses**: Complex scene — hard to render as a small icon. Could look like a children's book illustration.

---

## Concept B — The Fairy Ring

A ring of glowing mushrooms on a dark forest floor, seen from a low angle. The mushroom caps have subtle circuit-board trace patterns etched into their gills. A tiny winged sprite sits on each mushroom cap — 5 in total, one per mushroom. A faint Wi-Fi icon is carved into a stone at the center of the ring. Fireflies with LED-like glow float around.

**Vibe**: Folklore meets PCB. Magic that runs on silicon.

**Strengths**: Most distinctive. Directly references the folklore origin of "pillywiggins." The circuit-mushroom hybrid is memorable and memeable.

**Weaknesses**: May read as purely fantasy at small scales. The tech element (circuit traces) needs to be bold to survive scaling.

---

## Concept C — Stained Glass Emblem

A circular stained glass window in the style of a medieval guild hall. Five distinct figures stand around the perimeter — a trickster, a scholar, a healer, a guard, a messenger — each with their own pose, attribute, and color. In the center, a shared symbol: an open book with a gear, or a lantern, or a tree with roots connecting all five. The lead lines between the glass panes subtly form circuit paths.

**Vibe**: Timeless, guild-like, ecclesiastical but for a very secular purpose.

**Strengths**: Strongest icon potential. Works at 64×64 or 512×512. The stained-glass style is immediately recognizable. The lead-line-as-circuit motif ties tech in elegantly.

**Weaknesses**: Harder to convey "AI" or "home server" without explanatory text. Could feel too church-y.

---

## Concept D — The Constellation

Deep indigo field (or transparency). Five bright star-nodes arranged in a pentagon, each a different color: blue, purple, green, amber, grey. Fine gossamer lines connect each node to every other (or form the pentagon perimeter). Beneath the pentagon, a faint silhouette of a house or a small server rack — anchoring the constellation to something earthly. The lines between nodes have tiny dashes suggesting data or firefly trails.

**Vibe**: Clean, modern, abstract. Open source project logo.

**Strengths**: Simplest to render at any size. Works in monochrome. Easily recognizable as an icon. The pentagon is distinctive.

**Weaknesses**: Doesn't immediately say "fairy" or "agents." Could be a VPN or network monitoring tool.

---

## Concept E — The Fey Chip

A silicon microchip viewed from above, square and precise. The circuit traces on the die are shaped like fairy wings — organic, sweeping curves where you'd expect rigid right angles. Five contact pins/pads along the edges glow in different colors (one per agent/channel). In the die center, a tiny engraved emblem: an oak leaf or a mushroom. The surrounding PCB has a subtle Celtic knot border in the copper layer, revealing itself when you look closely — a second read for the curious eye.

**Vibe**: Hardware that's been enchanted. A chip you'd pull from a fairy's laptop.

**Strengths**: Clearest tech-meets-fae fusion. The chip is the council — distributed processing, interconnected, one device running your home. Multiple reads: quick glance = chip, second look = wings, third look = Celtic knot.

**Weaknesses**: Needs high detail to work. Simplified too far, it becomes just a generic chip icon.

---

## Concept F — The Noticeboard (bonus)

A cork noticeboard covered in pinned notes, sketches, and photos. Five distinct handwritings (one per agent) and five different ink colors on the notes. A few notes are pinned with string connecting them — shared knowledge. In the corner, a small sleeping cat or a steaming mug. A single glowing mushroom grows through a crack in the cork.

**Vibe**: Organized chaos. The council's shared workspace.

**Strengths**: Directly represents the council memory concept. Human and approachable.

**Weaknesses**: Very busy — terrible as an icon. More an illustration than a logo.

---

## Recommendation

| Use case | Best concept |
|----------|-------------|
| Repo avatar / favicon (64×64) | **C** (stained glass) or **D** (constellation) |
| Hero image / Open Graph card | **B** (fairy ring) or **A** (council ring) |
| README header | **E** (fey chip) |
| Twitter/social banner | **A** (council ring) |
| Overall strongest branding | **B** (fairy ring) — most distinctive, most memorable |

**If you can only make one**: Concept C (stained glass emblem) as the primary icon, Concept B (fairy ring) as the hero illustration.
